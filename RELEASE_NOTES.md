**3.18**

- **Lyrics show up, on time.** When the lyrics service was busy, MacSpaces fell back to lyrics without timing, so lines could appear many seconds late. It now waits for the timed ones, remembers lyrics it has found, and starts looking them up as the Nook opens. Lines change right as they're sung, and the scrubber no longer jitters.
- **Nicer lyric styles.** Karaoke fills with the singing, Typewriter types along with it, repeated lines replay, Poster keeps each line steady and readable, and Stickers appear only when a line really calls for one.
- **Smoother covers.** Skipping a song keeps the old cover until the new one is ready, instead of flashing a blank tile.
- **Notifications take you there.** A new message opens its conversation and a coding agent that needs you opens the Agents panel, unless you're using the Nook, when a card shows instead. Choose in Settings → Activities.
- **A better Tray.** Thumbnails for screenshots and files, clearer names, Clear All with Undo, and files that moved or were deleted handled quietly.
- **Mirror falls back.** Unplug your external camera and Mirror switches to the built-in one, then back when it returns.
- **Faster and lighter.** Less CPU when idle and on the Music page, and a smoother open and close.
