# MacSpaces 2.37

**2.37** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel. It replaces 1.14. On macOS 13 or 14, keep using 1.14.

**New in 2.x**

- **A dock of pages** under the Nook: Music, Calendar with year progress and a month grid, Weather, Reminders, Notes, Timers, Clipboard, System, Coding and Tray. Choose which appear.
- **Widgets in three sizes**, and more useful widgets: Join buttons for video calls, reminders you check off in the tile, timers with pause and +1 minute, one-tap Keep Awake, a second clock and a four-day forecast.
- **Themes:** MacSpaces, PowderMeet, Heartable, KemoSabe and Tsukumo, plus Noir, Rosé Pine, Kanagawa, Ayu and **Custom** colours.
- **Battery:** time left, health, cycle count and charger wattage, and an optional warning that tells Claude Code and Codex to save their work at 20%.
- **The notch:** moving sound bars while music plays, and a 6 pt gap below the camera.
- **Settings:** a to-scale Home editor, a Permissions page, and a window that fits any size.
- **Updates itself:** downloads each new version, checks the signature and that your Mac can run it, and asks before restarting.
- **Runs on macOS 15 and Intel Macs**, with an app icon that's rounded on every macOS version.
