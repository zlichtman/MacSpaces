# MacSpaces 2.35 (pre-release)

**2.35** is a development build for macOS 26 and Apple Silicon. The stable
release is 1.14; 2.35 does not replace it and installed 1.x copies are not
offered it as an update.

New since 2.34:

- **Themes:** Noir, Rosé Pine, Kanagawa and Ayu, plus **Custom**, where you pick the background and accent colours.
- **Low-battery warning for coding agents:** turn on System → Warn at 20%, and running Claude Code and Codex sessions are told to save their work and avoid long jobs while your Mac is on battery at 20% or below.
- **Battery:** time left or until full, battery health, cycle count and charger wattage.
- **Sound bars:** the closed notch shows moving bars again while music plays (still with Reduce Motion).
- **Settings:** fits tiled and narrow windows, with an icon-only sidebar below 720 pt.
- **Media:** the widget keeps one layout regardless of title length; lyrics and the album-art backdrop are smoother.

Everything in 2.x: a dock of pages (Music, Calendar with year progress, Weather, Reminders, Notes, Timers, Clipboard, System, Coding and Tray), Small/Medium/Large widgets, widget upgrades, Messages and Tsukumo quick bars, and a to-scale Settings editor.

Updates are paused in development builds. Download new pre-releases from GitHub
or run `brew upgrade --cask zlichtman/tap/macspaces@beta`.
