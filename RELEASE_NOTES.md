# MacSpaces 2.45

**2.45** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.45:

- **New Terminal widget.** Run quick commands right in the Nook: type `brew install …`, press Return, and the output appears below, colours and progress bars included. Swipe up on the command bar (or press ↑) for earlier commands. The command keeps running after the Nook closes, so reopen it to check on the result. Click the output to answer a prompt such as a password. Add it from Home → Add Widget → Tools.
- **A roomier Music page.** Lyrics get their own column beside the cover, with the playback controls under the artwork and the timeline alongside them. Long titles and lyrics wrap without moving the controls.
- **Mission Control no longer opens the Nook.** Reaching for the Spaces bar at the top of the screen won't open the Nook by hover or scroll; clicking the notch still does.
- **Lyrics on Home keep up.** The Media widget's lyric no longer gets stuck on the previous song until you open the Music page.
- **Smoother closing.** Closing the Nook no longer stops a page's services mid-animation, which could cause a brief stutter; they stay ready for a few seconds in case you reopen.
- When the Nook closes, your typing goes back to the app you were using.

The Terminal widget uses SwiftTerm by Miguel de Icaza (MIT licence).
