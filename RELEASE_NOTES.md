# MacSpaces 2.41

**2.41** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

New in 2.41:

- **A richer Media widget on Home:** it now matches the Music page, with the album art tinting the tile, the current lyric in your accent colour, a slim seek bar and the page's controls. No more "Media" label; the cover says it all.
- **No duplicate indicators:** volume and brightness changes are left to macOS's own on-screen indicators. The notch still shows microphone mute and Focus.
- **Smoother live effects:** theme effects, the sound bars and the Tsukumo characters no longer stop after sleep, display changes or long sessions.
- **Tidier themes:** the single-colour themes join Terminal (Custom stays last), and each drawer now flows by colour.
