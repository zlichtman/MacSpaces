# MacSpaces 2.65

**2.65** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.65:

- **Lyrics in every language.** Songs whose lyrics are filed under another spelling or script (a Russian artist written in Latin letters, accents left off) are found now; Russian, Spanish, German, French and the rest all work.
- **Uncensored lyrics.** MacSpaces never censored, but LRCLIB's community lyrics sometimes blank out words ("masochistic -"). Those words are now filled back in from the full lyrics, keeping the timing.
- **Over ninety stickers.** 45 more everyday things songs mention: streets, streetlights, cities, trains, buses, bikes, cigarettes, trees, leaves, beaches, clouds, guitars, pianos, headphones, TV, films, books, letters, doors, windows, sunglasses, clothes, shoes, umbrellas, beer, pills, hospitals, school, games, dice, painting, computers, radio signals, butterflies, fish, swimming, weekdays, dinner, hands, crowds, family, couches, sunsets and walks.
- **One sticker style.** Every sticker is 3D in its own natural colour, sized to fit beside the lyric without overlapping it.
- **Better sticker choices.** The line's headline word comes first, then concrete things, and only then common words like look, see or love: "made you smile and look away" is a smile.
- **Tidier Poster layout.** Longer endings sit under the headline instead of being squeezed into a narrow column beside it.

Also in 2.64:

- **Twice the stickers.** 24 new animated stickers for the Stickers lyric style: lightning, wind, sleep, coffee, drinks, birds, cats, dogs, crowns, keys, running, mountains, cameras, bells, gifts, balloons, diamonds, trophies, ideas, smiles, rainbows, boats, confetti and tornadoes.
- **Stickers with depth.** Every sticker is lit from above with a soft shadow, and the world is now a spinning 3D globe with turning continents, drifting clouds and an atmosphere.
- **A cleaner Settings sidebar.** The selected page is just highlighted, without the extra line beside it.
