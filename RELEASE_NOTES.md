# MacSpaces 2.47

**2.47** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.47:

- **Lyrics stay in time with the music.** The current line now follows the song closely instead of drifting behind it, and lines switch just as they're sung. The progress bar no longer slips back either.
- **Three lyric styles: Classic, Poster and Choreography.** If you'd chosen Stanza stage, Waterformed or Psychedelic bloom, lyrics return to Classic.
- **Fireworks replaces Pulse** in the Live themes: rockets climb and burst into sparks that drift down and twinkle out. If you used Pulse, you're switched to Fireworks.
