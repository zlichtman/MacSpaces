# MacSpaces 2.70

**2.70** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.70:

- **One Clock.** The World Clock is now the Clock's Dot style. The Clock has two options: Places (up to five, including where you are) and Style, Regular or Dot. A World Clock you'd added becomes a Dot clock, and an old second clock becomes your first place.
- **Every widget in Add Widgets.** Settings → Widgets → Add Widgets was missing the Clock's dot style, Calculator and Dev servers, and still listed Messages as in development; every widget is there now, in its group.
- **The thinking orb.** While a coding agent works, the notch shows a breathing thinking orb instead of three dots, and the ring and orb are Claude's orange or Codex's pale blue.

Also in 2.69:

- **Messages inbox.** A Messages page in the dock (add it in Settings → Widgets → Dock): your recent conversations, the open thread as bubbles, and a reply bar that sends to that exact conversation. It updates as messages arrive and reads nothing while it's closed.
- **Reply in the notch.** When a message comes in, click Reply and type your answer right in the card under the notch; it sends without opening Messages.
- **Agents that need you.** When Claude Code or Codex is waiting for you, a card comes down from the notch saying what it needs, and stays until it's working again; Go takes you to its app.
