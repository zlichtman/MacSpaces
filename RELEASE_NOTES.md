**4.25**

- **Shuffle.** A new button beside the theme filters: turn it on and the Nook opens on a different theme each time, drawn from the Dark or Light and pack you have selected.
- **Theme list.** The classics, starting with Catppuccin, now follow the scenes, with the plain colours after them. Peach is retired because it matched Apricot; if you use it, you keep it.
- **Clear the clipboard.** Folio's Clipboard has a Clear button again, for recent clips or everything including favourites.
