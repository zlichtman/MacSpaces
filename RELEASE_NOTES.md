# MacSpaces 2.40

**2.40** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel. On macOS 13 or 14, keep using 1.14.

New in 2.40:

- **Tsukumo in the notch:** a new Tsukumo page shows your Tsukumo bots as a crew of characters. Tag a few and talk to them together, open one to follow its coding task, answer its approvals and review its changes, all from the notch.
- **Safe by design:** approvals and accepting changes are explicit clicks, with no keyboard default; risky commands open in Tsukumo instead. What Tsukumo shows stays in memory on your Mac, and MacSpaces talks to Tsukumo only while the page is on screen.
- **Turn it on** in Settings → Widgets → Dock → Tsukumo. The page appears while an up-to-date Tsukumo is open.

Also in 2.x: fifty themes with live effects, a dock of pages, widgets in three sizes, a low-battery warning for Claude Code and Codex, and automatic updates.
