**2.91**

Lyrics searches keep valid matches when community records contain a missing or null duration, and skip malformed results individually.

Meetings and Dictation now open inside the Nook from its app bar. Both pages are added once to existing app bars and can be reordered or removed in Settings. Meeting controls use compact microphone and camera cards; Dictation provides an editable transcript with Start, Stop, Copy and Paste. Recording stops when its page closes, and Paste returns to the destination app.
