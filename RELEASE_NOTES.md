# MacSpaces 2.54

**2.54** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.54:

- **Meeting countdown.** A video meeting counts down beside the closed notch from ten minutes before it starts.
- **Coding agents in the notch.** Turn on Settings → Activities → Coding agents to see when Claude Code or Codex is working, needs you, or has just finished.
- **Choose where the sound goes.** The Music page has an output menu for speakers, headphones, AirPods and displays.
- **AirDrop from the Tray and baskets,** one file or all of them, with a click.
- **Hide in apps.** Pick apps (a game, a presentation) in front of which the Nook steps away.
- **Calculator widget.** Sums, units and currencies as you type; click the answer to copy it.
- **Dev servers widget.** Your local servers by port and project, to open or stop.
