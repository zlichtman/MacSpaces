# MacSpaces 2.55

**2.55** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.55:

- **Teleprompter.** A new page for your scripts, read just under the camera. It scrolls at your speed or follows your voice with on-device speech recognition, and it's hidden from screen sharing and recordings.
- **Notifications through the notch.** New Messages slide down under the camera with a Reply button, and you can also show other apps' notifications. Cards never appear in screen sharing or recordings. Settings → Activities → Notifications.
- **Messages works for real.** Out of development, with the text of new messages shown properly.
- **Screenshots land in the Tray,** with a thumbnail beside the notch and Copy Text for any image. Turn it on in Settings → General.
- **Timers with names, as many as you like,** and focus sessions that can turn a real Focus mode on and off with Shortcuts you pick.
- **Safer clipboard.** Card numbers and `.env` secrets are never recorded either.
- **A smoother File Converter wheel,** with soft corners and a whole ring when there's one choice.
- **Settings can grow** to show more, but never squashes smaller than it's designed for.
