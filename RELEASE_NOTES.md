# MacSpaces 2.69

**2.69** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel.

Changes in 2.69:

- **Messages inbox.** A Messages page in the dock (add it in Settings → Widgets → Dock): your recent conversations, the open thread as bubbles, and a reply bar that sends to that exact conversation. It updates as messages arrive and reads nothing while it's closed.
- **Reply in the notch.** When a message comes in, click Reply and type your answer right in the card under the notch; it sends without opening Messages.
- **Agents that need you.** When Claude Code or Codex is waiting for you, a card comes down from the notch saying what it needs, and stays until it's working again; Go takes you to its app.

Also in 2.68:

- **World Clock widget.** Your cities' times on a dot-matrix board ("06:28 LOS ANGELES"), with the unlit dots faintly showing, a ticking colon, your own city in the accent colour and minutes that flick over like a split-flap sign. Pick up to five cities in its settings.
- **A calmer agent notch.** No more text beside the notch: just the agent's app icon with a ring that sweeps round it while it works (pulsing orange when it needs you, a full green circle when it's done) and a matching animation on the other side, at the normal width. The full detail is in the Agents tab.
