A native Nook for music, weather, focus and files.

- One modern widget style with Midnight, Everforest and other coordinated themes.
- Arrange widgets into profiles, check weather on hover, and drag files through the Tray.
- Tray shows real previews of images, PDFs and video, and can copy, compress and share files.
- Battery details for your Mac and connected devices, with separate earbud and case levels.
- Bluetooth connection pop-ups are gone; the notch stays quiet when accessories connect.
- Spring-based motion: the notch peeks on hover, opens with depth and offers optional trackpad haptics.
- Settings organized around your widgets, appearance and activities.

**macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Signed and notarized**

Open **MacSpaces.dmg**, drag MacSpaces to Applications, and launch it. If MacSpaces
is already installed, replace that copy. Your profiles and settings are preserved.

This is MacSpaces **1.1**. MacSpaces 1.0.0 cannot install it automatically, so
download it once from this page; from 1.1 on, updates install from within the app.
