# MacSpaces 2.39

**2.39** is the current MacSpaces release, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon
and Intel. On macOS 13 or 14, keep using 1.14.

New in 2.39:

- **Live themes:** a new Live drawer with ten animated effects: Rainbow (a flowing RGB keyboard wave), Synthwave (a neon grid under a striped sun), Lava, Plasma, Code Rain, Warp, Pulse, Circuit, Prism and Nebula.
- **Nature comes alive:** petals drift down in Blossom, snow falls over Alpine, embers rise, tides move and the aurora shimmers. New: Monsoon (rain), Thunderstorm (rain and lightning), Firefly, Starlight and Autumn.
- **More themes:** Night Owl and Tomorrow Night join Terminal; Noir, Glass, Carbon, Cobalt and Acid move to a new Minimal drawer. Every drawer fills whole rows of five.
- **You stay in control:** effects move only while the Nook is open, at up to 30 frames a second, and hold still with Reduce Motion. Turn them off, or just their animation, in Settings → Appearance.
- The Sweets and Studio themes from 2.38 are retired; if you used one, MacSpaces picks the closest new theme.
- Tidier Settings: no colour dots on closed drawers, and the lyrics bar switch lives on the Nook itself.
