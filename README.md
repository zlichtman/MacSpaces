## Install

[Download MacSpaces.dmg](https://github.com/zlichtman/MacSpaces/releases/latest/download/MacSpaces.dmg), or:

```sh
brew install --cask zlichtman/tap/macspaces
```

## Requirements

- MacSpaces needs macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel
- Personal Voice needs macOS 14
- Voices from recordings need macOS 15, on Apple Silicon
- The on-device model needs macOS 26, with Apple Intelligence

## Studio and agents

- **Studio:** read scripts in any voice, keep the best take, prompt and dictate
- **Voices:** record your own or drop in recordings, music and all; each gets its best clip
- **Gateway:** Claude and Codex ask your on-device agent; your rules decide what it shares

<img src="Demos/screenshots/tape-poster.webp" alt="Tape: windows side by side in a strip you scroll, the focused one centred" width="100%">

- **Start:** turn on Tape in the MacSpaces menu, then ⌃⌥← and ⌃⌥→ step through windows
- **Per desktop:** leave Tape off on the desktops you'd rather arrange by hand

## Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no ads
- Access is asked for only when you turn a feature on
- Clipboard history and keys are encrypted on your Mac
- Folio reads pictures and PDFs on your Mac; Links fetch only pages you save
- Voices speak on your Mac; their models download from Hugging Face
- Weather and places use Open-Meteo, ipapi.co and Apple's geocoder
- Music uses LRCLIB, lyrics.ovh, Spotify, iTunes, Deezer and YouTube
- Currency uses frankfurter.app; updates and contributions use GitHub
- An anonymous count (a random ID and the version) goes to macspaces.dev; off in Settings
- Homebrew's tab runs `brew update` when you open it, at most hourly
- Spotify, Claude and Codex connect only with your own app, login or key
- Claude and Codex never see your calendar; your rules decide what's shared
