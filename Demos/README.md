# MacSpaces demo material

`2.x/screenshots/` holds the README's images. Each is a capture of the real app, rendered
by its Debug demo helpers (`Sources/Debug/WebsiteDemo234.swift`, `WebsiteDemoClips.swift`
and the FeatureQA scopes) with synthetic calendar, weather, clipboard, message and file
data, never the camera, clipboard or files of the Mac running them. They're the
website's captures, converted with `cwebp -q 84 -m 6`, and every one is referenced by
the README. Videos live on the website (zlichtman.com/macspaces), not here.

Songs and cover art belong to their artists and are shown only to demonstrate the
media controls; lyrics are short excerpts.

The page stills and Blossom Music hero were refreshed from MacSpaces 3.15 (build
116) on October 15, 2026. They show the shared app-page dimensions, current dock
and the four-ring System layout. Native screenshot fixtures supply sample battery
readings and the starter Home arrangement; no portfolio videos were re-recorded
or edited. Apple supplied the verified song metadata and cover art.
